About

My engineering philosophy centers on first-principles execution: understanding tolerances, structural mechanics, and material behavior before components hit the physical floor.

Through full-time engineering co-ops at DENSO and Honda, I have applied GD&T, CAD surfacing, and quality engineering loops to high-volume manufacturing environments. Outside the office, I design functional 3D-printed assemblies, tune chassis dynamics, and build microcontroller DAQ systems.
