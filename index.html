About

First-generation mechanical engineering student driven by a first-principles approach through understanding design, mechanics, and material behavior before components reach the production floor. My work centers on high-volume manufacturing, design, quality and maturation, tooling, and vehicle mechanical systems.

Through full-time engineering co-ops at Honda and DENSO, I have deployed CAD modeling, design, GD&T, and PDCA quality cycles within production environments. Outside the plant, I focus on rapid prototyping projects using 3D printing, mechanical systems design & testing, and microcontroller implementation.
